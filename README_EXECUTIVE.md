# Executive Summary – Cash Pooling Analytics PoC  
## Qlik Sense Cloud

---

## Purpose

This Proof of Concept demonstrates how **modern analytics and cloud governance** can be applied to support **executive decision-making in treasury**, specifically in evaluating the impact of **Cash Pooling**.

The project answers a single executive question:

> **Did Cash Pooling reduce idle cash and improve financial efficiency at group level?**

---

## Business Context

Cash Pooling is a widely adopted treasury practice, but its success depends on:
- Consistent implementation across entities
- Clear visibility of financial impact
- Executive trust in consolidated numbers

This PoC simulates a **multinational group environment**, with:
- Multiple companies and holdings
- Monthly cash positions
- Consolidation and group currency conversion

---

## What This PoC Delivers

### Executive-Focused Dashboard
- Clear **Before vs After Cash Pooling** comparison
- KPIs in **group currency**
- Monthly trend validation
- Consolidated view with drill-down to companies

### Governance by Design
- Controlled filters to protect analytical narrative
- Scenario comparison embedded in metrics
- Hierarchical consolidation aligned with real treasury models

### Decision-Ready Analytics
- No operational noise
- No technical dependency to interpret results
- Usable by a CFO in **under 3 minutes**

---

## What This PoC Is (and Is Not)

**This PoC is:**
- An executive impact assessment tool
- A reference architecture for cloud analytics
- A portfolio example of senior analytics thinking

**This PoC is not:**
- A treasury transaction system
- An operational cash management tool
- A forecasting or simulation engine

---

## Key Takeaway

> Analytics only creates value when it enables better decisions.

This project demonstrates how **analytics, governance and financial processes** can be combined to produce insights that executives can trust and act upon.
