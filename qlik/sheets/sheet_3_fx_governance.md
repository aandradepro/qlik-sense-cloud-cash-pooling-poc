# Sheet: FX / Governance

Focuses on **FX exposure and currency risk**.

## KPIs

-   Total Cash -- PRE (Reporting Currency)
-   Total Cash -- POST (Reporting Currency)

## Key Visuals

-   **FX Exposure Status (Pie Chart)**
    -   FX Exposed: cash_currency ≠ reporting_currency
    -   Not FX Exposed: cash_currency = reporting_currency
-   **FX Exposure by Document Currency and Scenario**
-   **Scenario Comparison Table**
-   **Average FX Rate Validation**

## Purpose

-   Separate FX exposure from business effects
-   Validate FX logic without exposing FX tables
-   Ensure scenarios are never aggregated
